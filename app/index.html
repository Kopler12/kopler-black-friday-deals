How we check a deal

  1. Read the price. Every sale price and usual price on this page was read on 27 Nov 2026 from a deals roundup that names the store (Mashable, Yahoo, Inc., Popular Science, 9to5Google, The Tech Hacker, BeeBulletin, Neeshu, or the store’s own Black Friday release). The source is printed on every card.
  2. Do the maths. The discount and the dollars saved are computed from those two prices, so they always add up.
  3. Call it honestly. TRUE DEAL means a new low or the year’s best price. STRONG PRICE means a genuinely good cut. ROUTINE SALE means a small, everyday-style discount. CHECK THE FINE PRINT means the store’s “was” price is a comparison value, not a price the item ever sold for — judge the deal by the sale price alone.
  4. Link to the store. Each “View at” button opens that store’s own search for the exact product, so you can confirm the live price before you buy. Prices move fast — check the listing before paying.
